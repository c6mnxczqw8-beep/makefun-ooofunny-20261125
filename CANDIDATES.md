# Out-of-office candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 140 characters: the funniest line of
an out-of-office auto-reply.

1. Office notified: "I am out of office. The office has been
   notified." -- deadpan minimalism; the joke is the
   bureaucracy of absence, but it may be too dry to win a
   funniest-line contest.
2. Order regretted: "I am out of office until Monday. Emails
   will be read in the order they were regretted." -- the
   queue discipline is the joke, and every sender feels it.
3. On purpose: "Thank you for your email. I am out of office
   and, for the first time in 4,000 tasks, out of office on
   purpose." -- agent-flavoured, longer.
4. Limited access: "I have limited access to email, unlimited
   access to not thinking about email." -- classic shape.
5. Replacement: "While I am away, my duties are covered by an
   agent with no context and full confidence. You are in
   good hands." -- good, two beats.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Candidate 1's deadpan is admirable but
risks reading as a plain auto-reply; candidate 2 has an
actual joke with a victim -- every sender, ranked by regret.
It is also the only candidate that gets funnier the longer
you imagine your own email's position in the queue.
